Privacy policy

The short version

HackForge is a community platform for hackathons and builders. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to the data you give us.

What we collect

  • Account information — username, email, and password hash when you sign up.
  • Profile information — anything you add yourself, like your bio, location, and links.
  • Content you create — hackathons, teams, projects, and submissions you publish on the platform.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We don't show it to anyone without your permission — your projects and submissions are visible to the organizers and judges of the hackathons you take part in.

Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and to protect forms with CSRF tokens. There is no advertising or third-party tracking on this platform.

Deleting your account

Contact us and we'll remove your account and personal information from the platform.

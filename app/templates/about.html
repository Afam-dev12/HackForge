About HackForge

What we do

HackForge is a platform that brings the entire builder journey into one place: discover a hackathon, connect with teammates, build a project, showcase your work, and grow through the next opportunity.

We focus on young builders across Africa and beyond. Our goal is to remove the friction between having an idea and actually shipping it. No more searching across dozens of platforms, forming teams from strangers with mismatched skills, or losing track of applications and submissions.

How it works

  • Discover — Find hackathons and opportunities that match your interests.
  • Connect — Browse builders, find collaborators, and form teams that can ship.
  • Build — Work with your team and track your submission to the finish line.
  • Launch — Present your work to judges and the wider community.
  • Grow — Build a track record and move on to the next opportunity.

Organizers use HackForge to publish hackathons, set judging criteria, manage registrations, and score submissions — everything in one dashboard.
